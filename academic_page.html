Our Academic Programme

At Focus International School, we provide a supportive and engaging learning environment for students from Nursery to Upper School. Our academic programme focuses on developing knowledge, creativity, confidence, communication skills and independent thinking.

We believe that every child has unique abilities. Our teachers encourage students to explore their interests while building strong academic foundations for their future education.

Our Academic Sections

Nursery

Our Nursery programme provides young learners with their first step into formal education through a fun, safe and stimulating environment.

  • Language Development
  • Early Mathematics
  • Creative Activities
  • Environmental Awareness
  • Music and Movement
  • Social Skills

The Primary Section helps students develop strong foundations in academic subjects while encouraging curiosity, creativity and confidence.
